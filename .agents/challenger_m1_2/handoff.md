## 2. Logic Chain

1. Requirement R1 specifies that `tests/test_voice/test_kokoro_voice.py` must verify that `KokoroVoiceProvider` outputs real voice audio on CPU and does not fall back to a continuous synthetic beep.
2. For an isolation test to be effective, its assertion criteria must strictly differentiate continuous sine wave tones from natural human speech.
3. Natural speech exhibits intermittent pauses between words/phrases (`pause_ratio > 0.05`), dynamic amplitude envelope variation (`rms_variance > 50.0`), and wide frequency distribution across formant bands (`spectral_entropy > 4.0`).
4. Synthetic 440 Hz beep audio is constant amplitude (flat RMS variance ~18.55), continuous without pause (pause ratio = 0.0), and concentrated at a single harmonic peak (spectral entropy ~0.0).
5. Empirical execution confirms that intentional injection of a 440 Hz synthetic beep into `test_kokoro_voice.py` triggers immediate test failure (`AssertionError`), whereas real Kokoro speech synthesis passes all acoustic assertions.

## 3. Caveats

- **Silence Edge Case**: Complete silence (all zero PCM samples) also fails the tests (fails `non_zero_count > 1000` as well as pause ratio and variance thresholds), as intended.
- **Inference Speed**: ONNX CPU inference takes ~0.3s to 1.5s per synthesized audio segment depending on system CPU load.

The acoustic assertions in `tests/test_voice/test_kokoro_voice.py` are robust, empirically validated, and strictly differentiate synthetic beep audio from real synthesized voice. The test suite correctly approves valid Kokoro speech output and rejects fallback 440 Hz synthetic beep audio.
